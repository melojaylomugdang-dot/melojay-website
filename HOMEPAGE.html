A little about Melojay 🌸

Hi! I'm Melojay A. Lomugdang, an Information Technology student at the University of Antique, Tario-Lim Memorial Campus. I'm currently part of BSIT 2A, exploring how technology can turn ideas into something useful and creative.

I'm interested in learning more about web design, coding, digital creativity, and the many possibilities that come with technology. Every project is a chance to learn something new, improve my skills, and become more confident in what I do.

Outside the world of code, this website reflects my love for dreamy colors, sakura flowers, and designs that feel personal. My journey is still unfolding, and I'm excited to see where curiosity and hard work will take me.
